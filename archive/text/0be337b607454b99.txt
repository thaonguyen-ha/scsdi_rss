MANILA, Philippines – Clothing brand Linya-Linya has launched a West Philippine Sea merchandise line in collaboration with the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP).
A part of the AFP’s information campaign, this collection, titled “Linya-Linya x West Philippine Sea: Karapat-Dagat Ipaglaban,” aims to raise awareness on the country’s struggle to protect our seas.
It also doubles as an effort for Filipinos to show solidarity in everyday contexts — in the form of daily wear like t-shirts and tote bags, and items like stickers, notebooks, and mugs.
Each item in the collection is plastered with the phrase “Karapat-Dagat Ipaglaban,” a portmanteau combining the word “seas” with “worthy,” roughly translating to “seas worth fighting for.”
Proceeds from the collaboration will go to the troops deployed to the West Philippine Sea, as well as the fisherfolk and the communities in the area.

“It requires a new language. It requires a partner who has mastered the art of speaking not just to Filipinos, but as Filipinos — with heart, with humor, and with an undeniable sense of patriotism. Our partnership with Linya-Linya is the answer,” said Colonel Enrico Ileto.
Linya-Linya, meanwhile, emphasized that their aim as a brand was to make complex national issues more relatable to the average Filipino, adding that this collaboration was a “call to arts,” in tandem with the AFP’s “call to arms.”
