Previously unreleased footage shows a WZ-7 high-altitude reconnaissance drone operated by a unit of the People's Liberation Army (PLA) Air Force conducting a deterrence patrol over Huangyan Dao in the South China Sea. The footage offers a closer look at the mission and marks the first public release of detailed footage of the patrol.
