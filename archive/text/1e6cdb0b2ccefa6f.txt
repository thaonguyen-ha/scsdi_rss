FUEL ASSISTANCE. Filipino fisherfolk receive fuel from Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources vessel MV Mamalakaya, with support from Philippine Coast Guard vessel BRP Malapascua, at Bajo de Masinloc in the West Philippine Sea on Tuesday (Oct. 27, 2026). The PCG and BFAR delivered fuel to Filipino fishermen under the Kadiwa ng Bagong Bayaning Mangingisda Program to help reduce their operating costs. (Photo courtesy of PCG)
MANILA – The Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) and the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) delivered fuel to Filipino fishermen at Bajo de Masinloc on Tuesday, Oct. 27, as part of the Kadiwa ng Bagong Bayaning Mangingisda (KBBM) Program.
In a statement on Wednesday, PCG spokesperson for the West Philippine Sea (WPS) Commodore Jay Tarriela said the mission used BFAR vessel MV Mamalakaya. PCG vessel BRP Malapascua (MRRV-4403) and PCG Islander 251 aircraft supported the operation.
The aircraft conducted a maritime domain awareness flight over the area to help ensure the safety of the fishermen and the BFAR vessel.
“Upon the arrival of MV Mamalakaya, Filipino fishing boats already operating in the traditional fishing ground, numbering 40 to 50, approached the vessel and lined up to receive fuel,” Tarriela said.
According to testimonials from fisherfolk, the assistance allows them to stay longer in the fishing ground and reduce operating costs amid high fuel prices.
The KBBM Program purchases fresh catch directly from Filipino fishermen at sea and provides them with fuel and ice to lower their operating costs. It also aims to sustain their presence in the WPS.
Tarriela said the strong turnout of fishing boats at Bajo de Masinloc shows that Filipino fishermen continue to exercise their traditional fishing rights in the area, as affirmed by the 2016 Arbitral Award.
PCG Commandant Vice Admiral Rommel Supangan directed the deployment of PCG surface and air assets to secure the mission.
Supangan also commended the crews of BRP Malapascua, PCG Islander 251 and MV Mamalakaya for the safe and successful conduct of the activity.
During its flight, Tarriela said the PCG aircraft monitored China Coast Guard and People's Liberation Army Navy vessels in the approaches to Bajo de Masinloc and a Chinese research vessel operating near the shoal.
He said the PCG continues to monitor and document the vessels' presence.
The mission was completed without any untoward incident.
“This mission was conducted in accordance with the guidance of President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. to promote food security and maritime development in the WPS, and in line with his commitment to continue supporting Filipino fishermen who make a living in these fishing grounds despite the challenges they face at sea,” Tarriela said. (Raymond Carl M. Dela Cruz/PNA)
