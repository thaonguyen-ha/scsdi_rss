PCG forces Chinese fishing vessel, 4 boats to leave Lawak Island waters
MANILA — The Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) drove away a Chinese fishing vessel and four small boats on Thursday for allegedly engaging in illegal fishing in the territorial waters of Lawak Island in Kalayaan, Palawan.
In a statement, PCG Spokesperson for the West Philippine Sea Rear Admiral Jay Tarriela said the operation was carried out by PCG personnel stationed on the island.
“Lawak Island is an integral part of the Municipality of Kalayaan, Province of Palawan. Under international law, the Philippines exercises full sovereignty over its territorial sea, where no foreign vessel may fish without authorization,” Tarriela said.

The operation began on Thursday morning with PCG personnel launching a rubber boat to document and drive away Chinese fishing vessel YUETAIYU 63168, which was anchored within the island’s territorial sea.
On arrival, the team found four small fishing boats scattered near the Chinese mother ship.
The mother ship did not respond to repeated radio challenges, even as the team circled it three times.
The team then drove away the four small boats.
The team monitored the small boats as they returned to the mother ship, which then weighed anchor and left the island’s waters.
The team returned to Lawak Island without incident.
“The deployment of small boats from a Chinese mother vessel to conduct fishing activities in these waters is a violation of Philippine laws, including the Philippine Fisheries Code and the Philippine Maritime Zones Act,” Tarriela said.
The operation comes days after the PCG repelled foreign vessels from Kota Island, also in the Kalayaan Island Group.
On Oct. 9, the PCG repelled Chinese Maritime Militia Vessel 9622 and its 22 sampans from Kota Island, where the vessel was also repelled on Sept. 28.
On Oct. 11, the PCG, the Philippine Navy, and the Philippine Marine Corps repelled five unauthorized foreign fishing vessels from the island.
“Under the leadership of PCG Commandant Admiral Ronnie Gil Gavan, the PCG continues to monitor (the vessel) for any further monitoring and will sustain its vigilance against unauthorized fishing and other intrusions by foreign vessels in the Kalayaan Island Group,” Tarriela said.

The operation was carried out in accordance with the guidance of President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.
In his fifth State of the Nation Address in July, the President vowed that the Philippines will not surrender or waver in defending its territory and maritime rights in the West Philippine Sea, in adherence to international law and the 2016 Arbitral Award. (Raymond Carl Dela Cruz/PNA)
