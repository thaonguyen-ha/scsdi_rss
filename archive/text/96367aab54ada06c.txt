Typhoon Rosal to exit PAR early Saturday — Pagasa
LAPU-LAPU CITY, Cebu — Typhoon Rosal may exit the Philippine Area of Responsibility (PAR) early Saturday morning, October 24, according to the state weather bureau Pagasa.
In its 5 a.m. bulletin on Friday, October 23, Pagasa said Rosal might gradually weaken within the next 24 hours before rapidly losing strength.
The typhoon may be downgraded to a severe tropical storm by Saturday morning and further weaken into a tropical storm by evening.

As of 4 a.m. Friday, Pagasa located Rosal 275 kilometers (km) east-northeast of Itbayat, Batanes, moving northwestward at 15 kilometers per hour (kph).
Rosal packed maximum sustained winds of 155 kph near its center and gusts of up to 190 kph.
Wind signals, gale warning up
Pagasa has raised Tropical Cyclone Wind Signals (TCWS) over several areas in Luzon as Rosal moves near the country.
As of 4 a.m. Friday, TCWS No. 2 was in effect over Batanes and the northeastern portion of the Babuyan Islands, including Babuyan, Panuitan, Calayan, and Camiguin islands.
TCWS No. 1 was raised over the rest of the Babuyan Islands, mainland Cagayan, northeastern Isabela, Ilocos Norte, and Apayao.

The affected areas in northeastern Isabela are Maconacon, Divilacan, Santa Maria, Cabagan, San Pablo, and Palanan.
Meanwhile, the northeasterly windflow and Rosal’s outer circulation are expected to bring strong to gale-force gusts to most of Luzon and Visayas, as well as Zamboanga del Norte, Misamis Occidental, Lanao del Norte, Camiguin, Davao Occidental, and Davao Oriental.
As of Friday morning, a gale warning was in effect over the seaboards of Northern Luzon and the eastern seaboards of Central and Southern Luzon.
LPA outside PAR
Pagasa is also monitoring a low-pressure area (LPA) outside PAR.
As of 3 a.m. Friday, the LPA was located 2,310 km east of northeastern Mindanao.
The LPA is not affecting the country’s weather conditions and is unlikely to develop into a tropical depression within the next 24 hours.
