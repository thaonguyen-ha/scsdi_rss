MANILA, Philippines — The Coast Guard District (CGD) of Palawan on Thursday, Nov. 12, continued the temporary suspension of sea travel for small vessels due to rough sea conditions.
In an advisory, the CGD-Palawan said vessels or watercraft with a gross tonnage (GT) of 15 and below were prohibited from sailing in the following areas:

Coast Guard Station (CGS) North Eastern Palawan
- Brgy. Bulalacao
- Brgy. Delian
- Brgy. Decabaitot
- Depalian Island
- Linapacan
CGS South Western Palawan
- Lucban Island
- Comiran Island
- Mangsee Island
- Onok Island
- Brgy. Melville
- Brgy. Indalawan
- Western Part of Mainland Balabac
Meanwhile, vessels and watercraft 3GT and below were also barred from sailing within the areas as follows:
CGS Southern Palawan
- area of responsibility of Rio Tuba
- area of responsibility of Buliluyan
- area of responsibility of Tagalango
CGS South Central Palawan
- Brooke’s Point
- Sofronio Española
The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical, and Astronomical Services Administration earlier reported in its 5 a.m. weather forecast that moderate to rough seas may affect the eastern sections of Luzon and Visayas.
According to the weather bureau, the northeast monsoon, or the amihan, may affect Northern and Central Luzon, while the easterlies may continue to bring rains and strong winds over the rest of the country on Thursday. /jpv
