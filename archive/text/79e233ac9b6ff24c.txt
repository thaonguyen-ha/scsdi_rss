Flash floods inundated several areas of the island of Phuket, following heavy rainfall overnight, officials said.
All three districts of the southern island province, including its capital, Phuket City, were impacted by the flooding.
Water levels started to recede by late morning, but some lower-lying areas remained flooded and small vehicles were advised to avoid those areas, according to the provincial deputy governor, Romdon Hayiawae.
He said some parts of the province were also hit with mudslides and officials were working to clear the debris to prevent it from falling into canals and obstructing waterways.
Photos and videos shared on social media show floodwaters flowing into residential areas and filling up several roads, making them impassable.
About 100 households were affected. There were no reports of casualties, Romdon said.
The Thai Meteorological Department warned of heavy and continuous rain in the southern region until tomorrow, which could trigger more flash floods and landslides.
Flooding has battered many parts of Thailand since mid-September and at least 83 people were reported killed as of yesterday, according to the Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation.
Bangkok was inundated in late September after days of torrential rain, forcing thousands to evacuate and causing severe disruptions. — AP
