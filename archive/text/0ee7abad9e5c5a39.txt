Dinagat Islands breaks ground on P164-M soil lab, ecotourism boardwalk
SAN FRANCISCO, Agusan del Sur — The provincial government of Dinagat Islands broke ground on Friday, Oct. 16, for two major infrastructure projects worth a combined P164 million aimed at boosting the local food security and ecotourism.
Gov. Nilo P. Demerey Jr. presided over the ceremonies in Barangay Llamera in Libjo town, marking the formal start of civil works for a P64.5-million Provincial Soil Laboratory and the P99.68-million Gaas Inlet boardwalk.
Funded under the National Soil Health Program of the Department of Agriculture’s Bureau of Soils and Water Management (DA-BSWM), the soil testing facility will provide diagnostic testing and scientific soil analysis directly to local agrarian communities.
The project follows a preliminary site inspection conducted by DA-BSWM technicians on Oct. 28 last year and the subsequent signing of a memorandum of agreement on Oct. 13 this year.
Demerey emphasized that establishing an in-house laboratory is central to Dinagat’s agricultural productivity drive and long-term food security agenda.
“Through this facility, we aim to improve soil fertility, increase crop yields, and anchor sustainable farming practices across our island communities,” Demerey said, expressing gratitude to the DA-BSWM for backing the province’s long-standing proposal.
Alongside the agricultural facility, officials also broke ground for the Gaas Inlet Eco-Tourism Development Project, which features a 1.71-kilometer long, 3-meter wide boardwalk and support facilities leading to a designated watersports zone in Barangay Llamera.
The tourism corridor is bankrolled by the Tourism Infrastructure and Enterprise Zone Authority (TIEZA), an attached agency of the Department of Tourism.
Provincial planners said both high-impact developments are intended to stimulate economic resilience by pairing scientific farm modernization with community-based ecotourism.
University of the Philippines President Angelo A. Jimenez joined the milestone event together with a delegation from the UP Philippine Energy Research and Policy Institute headed by Division Chief Dexter S. Pajarillo.
Also present were DA-BSWM Laboratory Services Division Chief Maribel R. Jalalon, Project Development Officer III John Vincent C. Viojan, members of the Dinagat Sangguniang Panlalawigan, department heads, and municipal and village officials of Libjo. /gsg
