Drivers in Bali face hours-long queues for petrol as shortages hit Indonesian resort island
Bali Governor Wayan Koster has blamed delivery problems, insisting that fuel supplies remain secure and sufficient to meet local demand.
Read more about this topic : https://sc.mp/6565a
Drivers queued for hours at petrol stations across Indonesia’s Bali on Tuesday as a fuel shortage on the Indonesian resort island entered a third day, disrupting traffic and daily activities. But authorities have blamed logistics problems rather than fuel supplies, as Bali’s governor said stocks remain secure and sufficient to meet local demand.
