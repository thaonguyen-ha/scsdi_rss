A second potential runway being built by China in the Zamora (Subi) Reef is causing concern among Filipino residents of the Kalayaan Group of Islands (Spratly Islands) for being ‘too close’ to Philippine shores. Subi Reef is located 25 kilometeres from Pag-Asa Island where about a hundred Filipinos live. Residents on Pag-Asa say they can see the tops of the cranes and lights on Subi Reef at night. Analysts believe the massive reclamation being undertaken in Subi reef is meant for a 3,000 meter-long runway and other military facilities. China has justified its active construction on several islands in the disputed South China Sea (West Philippine Sea), claiming they are meant to promote maritime and navigational safety, scientific research, and are also for military use. The Phililippines has lodged a formal complaint before an international tribunal, which China refuses to recognize.
