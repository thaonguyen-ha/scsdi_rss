Pagasa raises Signal No. 2 in Batanes as Super Typhoon Rosal moves away
MANILA, Philippines — Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal (TCWS) No. 2 was raised over Batanes as Super Typhoon Rosal (international name: Champi) moved east of northern Luzon on Wednesday morning, according to the state weather bureau.
In its 11 a.m. bulletin on Wednesday, the Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical, and Astronomical Services Administration (Pagasa), the center of Rosal was last located 845 kilometers east of Northern Luzon as of 10 a.m.
It maintained its strength as it was still carrying a maximum wind speed of 215 kilometers per hour (kph) and gustiness of up to 265 kph. It slightly decelerated from 30 kph to 25 kph as it was moving west northwestward.
As Batanes was placed under TCWS No. 2, Pagasa warned of potential minor to moderate threat to life and property where wind speeds range from 62 to 88 kph. It added that Batanes will experience 50 to 100 millimeters of rainfall from Wednesday to Friday noon.
Pagasa on Tuesday said that the northern areas of Luzon, particularly Batanes, will be most affected by Super Typhoon Rosal. It also said that Rosal will be the closest to extreme northern Luzon between Thursday and Friday afternoon.
Meanwhile, TCWS No. 1 was hoisted over the following areas where minimal to minor threat to life and property may be possible, as wind speeds range from 39 kph to 61 kph.
- Cagayan including Babuyan Islands
- Eastern portion of Isabela (Maconacon, Divilacan, Palanan, Santa Maria, Cabagan, Delfin Albano, Santo Tomas, Tumauini, San Pablo, Ilagan City, San Mariano, Dinapigue)
- Ilocos Norte
- Apayao
- Northeastern portion of Catanduanes (Pandan, Bagamanoc, Panganiban, Viga, Gigmoto)
The bureau also shared that Rosal will continue moving west northwestward on Wednesday, then northwestward by Thursday. It will then move north northwestward by Friday before recurving northeastward by Sunday.
Rosal is expected to exit the Philippine area of responsibility by Friday evening or early Saturday morning. /jpv
