Navy reservist Rear Adm. Roy Vincent Trinidad, AFP spokesperson for the WPS, said in an interview with PTV's Bagong Pilipinas Ngayon Wednesday.
Despite China’s overwhelming maritime presence, the Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP) vowed to step up efforts to protect and uphold the country’s sovereignty and sovereign rights in the West Philippine Sea (WPS).
"But in spite of all of this, hindi tayo pinanghihinaan ng loob (we will not lose our resolve), we will match them hangga’t kaya natin (as long as we can), ship for ship, patrol for patrol. yung mga maling naratibo nila, kinocorrect natin sa tamang naratibo (their wrong narratives, we will correct it with the right ones)," Navy reservist Rear Adm. Roy Vincent Trinidad, AFP spokesperson for the WPS, said in an interview with PTV's Bagong Pilipinas Ngayon Wednesday.
Earlier, the AFP reported that some 101 Chinese naval and coast guard vessels were spotted in four key areas in the WPS last September, more than double the 56 detected in August.
Trinidad also clarified that even when there are many Chinese ships sighted in the WPS it does not automatically mean that there is no equivalent Philippine presence.
"That's why kung makita nyo (if you will see), may mga (there are) coercive and aggressive actions na ginagawa laban sa atin (being conducted against our presence)," he added.
Trinidad said the AFP will continue to monitor the entire expanse of the country's maritime domain as this will provide them with the information on how to counter this illegal Chinese presence.
"We will keep monitoring them, it will also give us an input kung saan (on where to deploy) ang ating mga barko at mga eroplano (our ships and aircraft) to be able to counter them," he added. (PNA Priam Nepomuceno)
