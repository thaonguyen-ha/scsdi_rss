INQToday: Trade deals, Asean protest arrests, South China Sea code
MANILA, Philippines —Here are INQToday’s top stories this afternoon.
The Department of Foreign Affairs said the next steps toward finalizing the long-awaited agreement among ASEAN member states and China are now in Singapore’s hands. Meanwhile, the Department of Trade and Industry declined to disclose whether new trade agreements with the United States would be discussed during US President Donald Trump’s visit to the Philippines for the 49th ASEAN Summit.
Also, police arrested hundreds of demonstrators who joined protests against Trump’s visit and other regional issues, with clashes breaking out as protesters attempted to march toward the summit venue./dp
