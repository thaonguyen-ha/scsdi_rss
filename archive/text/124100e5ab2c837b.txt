A vessel formation of the China Coast Guard (CCG) on Monday conducted a patrol in China's territorial waters of Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands, according to a statement by the CCG.
The patrol was carried out in accordance with the law to protect China's legal rights in the area, the statement said.
