Beijing drills water cannons and drones near disputed South China Sea shoals
Training off Second Thomas and Sabina shoals tests small boats, surveillance drones and boarding tactics as run-ins with Manila continue

The Chinese coastguard has carried out an exercise featuring water cannons and drones near two disputed reefs in the South China Sea to prepare for “foreign infringement”, according to state broadcaster CCTV.
A China Coast Guard (CCG) flotilla “recently” conducted “comprehensive maritime law-enforcement training” in the waters around Second Thomas Shoal and Sabina Shoal, CCTV said on Friday, without specifying when these took place.
The two shoals – part of the disputed Spratly Islands – are at the centre of Beijing’s simmering South China Sea tensions with rival claimant the Philippines.
