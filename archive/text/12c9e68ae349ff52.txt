ASEAN leaders to discuss Myanmar and South China Sea, hold special meeting with Trump
ASEAN is set to review the state of ties with Myanmar when the bloc convenes its final summit this year in Manila. Leaders from ASEAN's dialogue partners will also be present, including the prime ministers of Japan, India and Australia. The White House has also confirmed that US President Donald Trump will hold a special meeting with ASEAN on Tuesday. Afifah Ariffin reports from Manila.
