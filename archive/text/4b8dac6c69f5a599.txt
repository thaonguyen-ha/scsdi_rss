Beijing looks forward to early conclusion of South China Sea code of conduct: Li Qiang
China has described relations with ASEAN as "extraordinary", as it expresses support for an early conclusion of the South China Sea code of conduct. Chinese Premier Li Qiang was speaking at a summit with ASEAN leaders. Officials are set to miss the year-end deadline, despite having provisionally agreed on 80% of the draft of the code. Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Junior has pledged to maintain the search for common ground and practical cooperation with Beijing. Tan Qiuyi reports from Manila.
