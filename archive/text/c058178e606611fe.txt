China’s Liaoning carrier and USS Theodore Roosevelt may be in Philippine Sea at same time
Northeastward course could see Liaoning group sail close to waters where US, Japan and other allies are carrying out Keen Sword exercise

Albee Zhangin Shanghai
China’s Liaoning aircraft carrier strike group sailed beyond the first island chain last week to reach the southernmost waters of the Philippines, suggesting an intent to position itself closer to ongoing US-Japan joint exercises.
Philippine news channel BNC reported that the carrier group reached about 25 nautical miles (46km) east-southeast of Balut Island, the southernmost point of the Philippines, before sailing northeast on Friday.
That positioned the warships past the first island chain, a US-led strategic arc of maritime chokepoints running from Japan through Taiwan to the Philippines.
