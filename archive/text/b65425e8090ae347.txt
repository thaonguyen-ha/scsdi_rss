MANILA: The Philippine Coast Guard District (PGD) of Palawan said sea travel for small boats was temporarily suspended on Wednesday (Nov 11), due to the effects of the northeast monsoon, or amihan.
The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical, and Astronomical Services Administration earlier issued a gale warning over the northern and eastern seaboards of Northern Luzon due to rough to very rough seas brought by the amihan. - Philippine Daily Inquirer/ANN
