Five stories. Five different ways of living with the sea. Across the islands and fishing communities of the South China Sea, fishermen and their families share lives shaped by the ocean. From finding fresh water on a remote island and navigating routes passed down through generations, to diving into reefs, exploring new fishing grounds and waiting for boats to return. Their lives are changing. So are the waters they depend on. But the sea remains a place of work, memory and belonging. Discover the people and stories behind "The Sea We Call Home," only on CGTN.
