Pagasa: Isolated rains possible in most of PH on Tuesday
MANILA, Philippines — Metro Manila and the rest of the country may experience isolated rain showers on Tuesday, Nov. 17, with the Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration (Pagasa) saying that the easterlies are still prevailing over the country.
Pagasa, in its 5 p.m. weather forecast, said the Amihan, or northeast monsoon, is not affecting the country.
However, it said scattered thunderstorms may still be experienced over parts of Caraga and Davao Regions.

Over Batanes, cloudy skies may be experienced, while isolated rain showers are still possible in the rest of the country, including Metro Manila.
“Meanwhile, in the Batanes area, the skies will be cloudy, while for the rest of the country, including Metro Manila, isolated rain showers, lightning, and thunder are still possible,” said Pagasa weather specialist Grace Carpio.
“Mataas yung temperatura na ating mararanasan at possible pa rin ‘yong mga scattered na pag-ulan, pagkulog at pagkidlat sa bahagi ng Caraga at Davao Region. Dala pa rin ito ng Easterlies,” Carpio added.
(High temperatures are expected to persist, while scattered rain showers and thunderstorms may still occur over parts of the Caraga and Davao regions due to the easterlies.)
